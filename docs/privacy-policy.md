# Privacy Policy for Tarantado

**Effective date:** 19 August 2026
**Last updated:** 19 August 2026

Tarantado is a music manager for classic portable audio players. It runs on
your iPhone, iPad, or Mac and copies music from your device to a player you
connect over USB.

**Tarantado does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.** The app
has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising. It makes no
network connections of any kind.

## What Tarantado stores on your device

Everything Tarantado works with stays on your own hardware. Specifically:

- **Music files you import.** These are copied into Tarantado's own folder on
  your device so they remain available whether or not a player is connected.
  This folder is visible to you in the Files app, so you can add music to it or
  take music back out at any time.
- **A reference to the player you last connected.** When you pick a connected
  player, the operating system gives Tarantado a bookmark to that location so
  it can reconnect after you reopen the app. This bookmark is stored in the
  app's own settings. It is a reference to a location, not a copy of its
  contents.
- **Information read from a connected player.** To confirm it is writing to a
  device it understands, Tarantado reads the player's model, serial number, and
  hardware identifiers from the player itself, and shows them to you on the
  Device screen. This information is displayed only. It is never stored by the
  app and never leaves your device.

## What Tarantado does not do

- It does not collect personal information.
- It does not transmit anything over the internet.
- It does not use analytics, tracking, advertising, or crash-reporting
  services.
- It does not include third-party SDKs or code libraries that collect data.
- It does not require an account, a login, or an email address.
- It does not read your music library metadata for any purpose other than
  showing it to you and writing it to the player you chose.

## Permissions

Tarantado asks for access only to files and folders you explicitly select, and
only when you select them. It cannot browse your device on its own. Access is
granted through the standard system file picker, and you can revoke it at any
time through your device's settings.

## Data retention and deletion

Because nothing is collected, there is nothing for Tarantado to retain or for
you to request deletion of. Music you import lives in Tarantado's folder and
can be deleted from within the app or through the Files app. Deleting the app
removes its folder, its stored settings, and everything in them.

## Children's privacy

Tarantado does not collect personal information from anyone, including children
under 13.

## Information Apple may collect

Tarantado itself sends nothing anywhere. However, when you install the app
through the App Store or TestFlight, Apple may collect information about the
download and, if you have opted in to sharing analytics in your device
settings, may share crash logs and usage statistics with the developer. That
data is collected by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy, not by Tarantado,
and it is aggregated rather than tied to your identity. See
[Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) for details.

## Changes to this policy
